# Translations

## Vietnamese (`src/locales/vi.json`)

The Vietnamese strings in this app were **generated by AI (Claude, Anthropic)**
on 2026-08-27 and **have not been reviewed by a native speaker**. Treat them as
a machine translation: the meaning should be right, but register, terminology
and phrasing may not be what a native Vietnamese speaker — in particular a
Vietnamese-Australian home buyer — would actually say.

The same disclosure ships in two other places:

- a `_meta` block at the top of `vi.json` (stripped before the strings reach
  i18next);
- a dismissible notice shown in the UI while the Vietnamese locale is active.

## Terms deliberately left in English

Brand, program and institution names, and financial terms with no common
Vietnamese equivalent, are kept in English (usually with a Vietnamese gloss on
first use): **Cash Before You Bid**, **AUD**, **LMI**, **LVR**, **PEXA**,
**APRA**, **Help to Buy**, **First Home Owner Grant**, **5% Deposit Scheme**
(translated descriptively as "Chương trình đặt cọc 5%"), **strata**,
**off-the-plan** (glossed as "mua nhà trên giấy"), **Land Services Victoria**,
**CBA**, **Bank Australia**, place names.

**State Revenue Office** is also kept in English, in the "rates current as at"
line beside the results (`notes.ratesAsAt*`): it is the institution's own name
and what a buyer will see on its website.

Legislation is named in English, because that is what a buyer will see on the
contract and what an agent or conveyancer will say: **Section 27** and
**Sale of Land Act 1962** (in `guidance.bondBody`, kept together as
"Section 27 của Sale of Land Act 1962"). The same rule covers the payment
terms in that note: **deposit bond** (glossed as "giấy bảo lãnh đặt cọc"),
**bank cheque**, **EFT**, **personal cheque**, **contract of sale**,
**cooling-off period** (glossed as "thời gian được rút lui khỏi hợp đồng") and
**conveyancer**.

Terms a native reviewer should look at first: "phương án" (saved scenario —
chosen over the more literal "kịch bản", which reads as a film script),
"thuế trước bạ" (stamp duty),
"tất toán" (settlement), "phí pháp lý chuyển nhượng" (conveyancing),
"người bảo lãnh" (guarantor), "vốn chính phủ" (government equity),
"khoản dự phòng" (buffer), "trả giá" (bid). Alternatives are listed in the
pull request that introduced the localisation.

From the auction-day deposit guidance (`guidance.*`) and the deposit hints
(`hints.*`), in rough order of how likely they are to be wrong:

- "bên môi giới" for the selling agent — "đại lý bất động sản" and
  "công ty môi giới" are the alternatives, and the right one depends on how
  Vietnamese-Australian buyers actually refer to the agent at an auction.
- "giải ngân sớm" for the early release of a deposit under Section 27
  ("giải toả sớm" is the other candidate).
- "búa gõ chốt giá" for the fall of the hammer — deliberately literal.
- "giấy bảo lãnh đặt cọc" as the gloss for a deposit bond.
- "chuyên viên pháp lý chuyển nhượng" for conveyancer, which follows the
  existing "phí pháp lý chuyển nhượng" for conveyancing.
- "thông lệ phổ biến" for "common practice", in the line that keeps the note
  descriptive rather than advisory.

## Submitting corrections

1. Edit `src/locales/vi.json` (or `en.json`). Keys are flat or one level deep;
   `{{placeholders}}` must be kept exactly as they are — they are filled in at
   runtime with locale-formatted numbers.
2. Do not change the `_meta` block except to record a review: once a native
   speaker has checked the file, set `"reviewed_by_native_speaker": true` and
   add your name/date.
3. Run `npm test` (the suite renders the app in both locales) and open a pull
   request, or an issue quoting the key and the suggested wording if you would
   rather not touch the code.
