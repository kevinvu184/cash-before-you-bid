01 — Your numbers

A$600,000A$750,000
  • Exemption ends. Up to A$600,000 an eligible first home buyer pays no land transfer duty at all. Past it the exemption is gone and duty phases in, rising far more steeply with price than it does anywhere else.
  • Concession ends. Between A$600,000 and A$750,000 the duty is scaled by how far into the band the price sits, climbing from nothing to the full amount across it. From A$750,000 the concession is gone and the full duty applies.
The contract sets the deposit you pay on the day, usually 10%. The scheme needs at least 5%.
